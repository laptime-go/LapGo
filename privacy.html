Privacy Policy for LapTime Go / 圈速go

Last updated: 2026-08-09

1. Overview

LapTime Go does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. All GPS timing data is processed locally on your device.

2. Location Data

The app uses GPS location (foreground and background) solely for lap timing functionality. Location data is used to detect start/finish lines and calculate lap times. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with any third party, except as required for map display and weather API (Open-Meteo) which only uses approximate lat/lng to fetch temperature.

3. Background Location

Background location is required to continue lap timing when the screen is off or the app is in background during track sessions. A persistent notification "圈速go 正在計時" is shown when background tracking is active.

4. Data Storage

Lap history is stored locally using AsyncStorage on your device. You can delete it anytime in Settings > Clear All.

5. Advertising

We use Google AdMob (react-native-google-mobile-ads). AdMob may collect device advertising ID as per Google's policy. No personal location is sent to AdMob by us.

6. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or precise location history on any server.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13.
