Privacy Policy for LapTime Go / 圈速go

Last updated: 2026-09-26

1. Overview

LapTime Go does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our own servers. All GPS timing data is processed locally on your device. However, third-party SDKs (Google AdMob, Open-Meteo) may collect data as described below.

2. Location Data - Foreground Only

The app uses GPS location ONLY when the app is in foreground (open on screen) solely for lap timing. Location is used to detect start/finish lines and calculate lap times. If you switch apps or turn screen off, timing will pause. This is intentional to comply with Google Play foreground-only policy. Precise location is processed locally and not stored on our server. Only approximate lat/lng is sent to Open-Meteo weather API to fetch temperature.

3. Background Location

We do NOT use background location. We do NOT request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permission. No background tracking, no persistent notification.

4. Data Storage

Lap history is stored locally using AsyncStorage on your device. You can delete it anytime in Settings > Clear All.

5. Advertising & Data Sharing

We use Google AdMob (react-native-google-mobile-ads). AdMob may collect Device Advertising ID and App Interaction data for advertising purposes as per Google's policy. No precise location is sent to AdMob by us.
We use Open-Meteo Weather API. It receives approximate location only.

6. Data Collection Summary (for Google Play Data Safety)

- Precise Location: Collected for App Functionality, shared with Open-Meteo, encrypted in transit, required for app functionality.
- Device or other IDs (Advertising ID): Collected for Advertising, shared with AdMob, encrypted in transit.
- App Activity (App Interactions): Collected for Advertising, shared with AdMob, encrypted in transit.

7. No Personal Data Collection On Our Server

We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or precise location history on any server owned by us.

8. Data Deletion & Your Rights

How to delete your data:
1. Local data: Open LapTime Go > Settings > Clear All, or go to Android Settings > Apps > LapGo > Storage > Clear Data, or uninstall the app.
2. Third-party data: To request deletion of data collected by AdMob or Open-Meteo, please contact us at fungfung1984@gmail.com. We will respond and process your request within 7 days.
3. Deletion request webpage: https://laptime-go.github.io/LapGo/privacy.html
After deletion, no data is retained.

9. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13.

10. Contact

Contact: fungfung1984@gmail.com
