Privacy Policy for LapTime Go / 圈速go

Last updated: 2026-09-26

1. Overview

LapTime Go does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. All GPS timing data is processed locally on your device.

2. Location Data - Foreground Only

The app uses GPS location ONLY when the app is in foreground (open on screen) solely for lap timing. Location is used to detect start/finish lines and calculate lap times. If you switch apps or turn screen off, timing will pause. This is intentional to comply with Google Play foreground-only policy. Location data never leaves your device and is not shared with any third party, except Open-Meteo weather API which only uses approximate lat/lng to fetch temperature.

3. Background Location

We do NOT use background location. We do NOT request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permission. No background tracking, no persistent notification.

4. Data Storage

Lap history is stored locally using AsyncStorage on your device. You can delete it anytime in Settings > Clear All.

5. Advertising

We use Google AdMob (react-native-google-mobile-ads). AdMob may collect device advertising ID as per Google's policy. No location is sent to AdMob by us.

6. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or precise location history on any server.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13.
